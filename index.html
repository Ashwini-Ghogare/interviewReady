Practice Interviews with AI.

InterviewReady helps final-year students practice interview questions, improve their answers, and prepare with confidence.

The Problem

Preparing alone can make interview practice difficult.

Students may know their projects and technical topics, but they can still struggle to answer clearly, handle follow-up questions, and identify areas that need more practice.

Why Practice with InterviewReady?

1. Realistic Interview Questions

Practice common HR, behavioral, project, and technical questions in a structured interview-style session.

2. Clearer Answers

Practice making your explanations more concise, structured, and easier to communicate.

3. Focused Preparation

Identify areas that need more practice and prepare with a clear focus.

About InterviewReady

InterviewReady is a proposed interview-practice tool designed to help final-year students prepare with structured practice.

Practice Areas

Prepare for different parts of a real interview in one place.

01

HR Round

Practice introduction, strengths, weaknesses, career goals, and common HR questions.

02

Technical Round

Revise programming, database, framework, and role-specific technical questions.

03

Project Round

Practice explaining your project, technologies, responsibilities, and challenges clearly.
